sMeetSec — PRIVACY NOTICE
Version 1 · simply handle IT

Short version: sMeetSec does not collect, send or sell any data. Meetings stay on this computer.

- sMeetSec works offline. It does not create accounts, show ads, track usage or contact any
  server. The app talks only to itself on this computer (127.0.0.1): its engine, its
  built-in translation engine (llama.cpp) or, if you choose it, Ollama on this computer.
- Internet is used only when you press Models > Download (Hugging Face and GitHub, to fetch
  the speech, speaker and translation models). You start that yourself; nothing about you
  or your meetings is sent.
- What is stored, and where:
  · recordings, transcripts, translations and reports: data\meetings\ in the sMeetSec folder;
  · settings: data\settings.json;
  · desktop window data (theme): %LOCALAPPDATA%\sMeetSec.
  Delete a meeting in the app (🗑) or delete the folders above to remove everything.
- Live mode uses the microphone, and system audio only when you choose it and confirm the
  Windows / browser share dialog. Audio is processed on this computer only.

RECORDING PEOPLE: tell meeting participants that the meeting is recorded and transcribed,
and follow your company rules and Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Recordings
and transcripts can contain personal data; keep them only as long as needed.
