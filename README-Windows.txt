sMeetSec 0.3 - simply handle IT Meeting Secretary (Windows)
============================================================

1. Double-click sMeetSec.exe   (no Python or Ollama needed)
2. First start: the Models window opens - press "Download missing models"
   (internet needed once; about 2-5 GB depending on the PC).
   PC without internet: copy the "models" folder from another PC instead.
3. Done. Everything runs on this computer; meetings are saved in data\meetings.

Tell participants that the meeting is recorded (PDPA).
